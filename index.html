Trends

The 12-week trend line is fitted to your rolling 7-day total. If it changes by less than this share of your average week, the trend shows as steady. The same applies to the effort trend. Your weekly effort range comes from your average over the previous 3 full weeks. These are your own heuristics, not medical limits.

VO2max decimals: the app downloads each outdoor run's original file from Intervals.icu once (about 0.3 MB per run, only for the period you are viewing) and keeps just the number.
